When Kajabi is actually better

Kajabi wins if you need:

  • • Built-in email marketing with automations and sequences
  • • Landing pages and sales funnels without external tools
  • • A full website hosted on your custom domain
  • • Quizzes, assessments, and certificates in courses
  • • An all-in-one marketing platform (not just community + courses)

If you're using all of Kajabi's marketing features, switching to Skool means you'll need separate tools for email (ConvertKit/Brevo), landing pages (Carrd/Leadpages), and your website.

How to migrate

  1. 1
    Export everything from Kajabi. Download course videos, PDFs, member list (CSV), email list, and any resources. Do this before canceling anything.
  2. 2
    Set up your email tool first. Since Kajabi has email and Skool doesn't, get ConvertKit or Brevo set up and import your email list before you start the switch.
  3. 3
    Create your Skool community. Sign up here and follow our main guide to build out your community.
  4. 4
    Rebuild your courses in Skool. Upload videos to Classroom. Skool hosts natively. Simplify if your Kajabi course structure was complex.
  5. 5
    Set up a landing page. If you need a sales page, create one on Carrd ($19/year) or Leadpages. This replaces Kajabi's built-in pages.
  6. 6
    Migrate members. Email your list about the move. Highlight what's better: gamification, live calls, simpler experience, lower cost (if you're passing savings to them).
  7. 7
    Run parallel for 30 days. Keep Kajabi active during transition. Redirect all new purchases to Skool. Once members have moved, cancel Kajabi.
