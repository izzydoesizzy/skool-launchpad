Switching from Circle to Skool

Circle is a solid platform. But if you're here, you're probably looking for simpler pricing, better gamification, or Skool's built-in discovery. Here's how to make the switch.

Why people switch from Circle to Skool

$
Simpler pricing. Circle starts at $39/mo and scales to $219/mo. Skool is $99/mo flat for everything on Pro, or $9/mo to start on Hobby.
★
Native gamification. Skool's points, levels, and leaderboards are core to the platform. Circle added gamification in 3.0 but it's not as deeply integrated.
🔍
Built-in discovery. Skool has a marketplace where people find and join communities. Circle doesn't. This means free organic traffic on Skool.
⚡
Simplicity. Skool does fewer things but does them well. If Circle's feature set feels like overkill for your coaching business, Skool is the antidote.

How to migrate (step by step)

  1. 1
    Export your Circle content. Download your course materials, member list (CSV), and any resources. Circle lets you export member data from Settings.
  2. 2
    Create your Skool community. Sign up here and follow our main guide to set up your new community.
  3. 3
    Rebuild your courses. Re-upload videos and content into Skool's Classroom. Skool hosts video natively, so no need for external hosting.
  4. 4
    Set up an email tool. Since Skool doesn't have email, connect ConvertKit, Brevo, or Mailchimp via Zapier. Set up a welcome email automation.
  5. 5
    Run both platforms for 2-4 weeks. Don't kill Circle immediately. Run them in parallel. Post in both. Let members migrate naturally.
  6. 6
    Invite your Circle members. Email your member list with the new Skool link. Explain why you're moving and what they'll gain (gamification, live calls, simpler experience). Offer a migration incentive if needed.
  7. 7
    Sunset Circle. Once 80%+ of active members have moved, cancel your Circle subscription.
