Switching from Mighty Networks to Skool

Mighty Networks is powerful, especially if you need a branded app. But if you want simplicity, better gamification, and built-in discovery, Skool might be the move.

Why people switch from Mighty Networks to Skool

⚡
Simpler to manage. Mighty Networks has a lot of features and configuration options. Skool strips it down to four things: Community, Classroom, Calendar, Chat. Less to manage, less to confuse your members.
★
Better gamification. Mighty has limited gamification. Skool's points, levels, and leaderboards are front-and-center, driving engagement automatically.
🔍
Built-in discovery. Skool has a marketplace where new members can find your community. Mighty Networks doesn't. Free organic traffic is a real advantage.
$
Predictable pricing. Mighty's pricing scales as you grow and add features. Skool is $99/mo flat on Pro regardless of community size.

How to migrate

  1. 1
    Export your content. Download course videos, documents, and your member list from Mighty Networks.
  2. 2
    Create your Skool community. Sign up here and follow our main guide.
  3. 3
    Rebuild your courses. Upload videos to Skool's Classroom. It hosts natively with auto-captions. Simplify your course structure if Mighty's was complex.
  4. 4
    Run parallel for 2-4 weeks. Keep Mighty active while you build out Skool. Let members experience both before you sunset Mighty.
  5. 5
    Migrate members. Email everyone with the new link. Highlight what's new and better. Offer a migration bonus or discount.
  6. 6
    Cancel Mighty Networks. Once your active members have moved, you're done.
