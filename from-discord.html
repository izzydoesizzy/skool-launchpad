🎯

When Discord is actually better

Be honest with yourself. Stay on Discord if:

  • • Your community is free and you don't plan to charge
  • • Real-time chat is essential (gaming, crypto, dev communities)
  • • Your audience is already deeply embedded in Discord
  • • You rely heavily on bots and automation

How to migrate

  1. 1
    Create your Skool community. Sign up here and follow our main guide to set everything up.
  2. 2
    Build your course content. Take the best resources, guides, and pinned messages from your Discord and turn them into organized Classroom lessons.
  3. 3
    Announce the move in Discord. Don't just disappear. Explain why you're upgrading: better organization, courses, live events, gamification. Frame it as an upgrade, not an abandonment.
  4. 4
    Offer early access. Give your most active Discord members first access to Skool with a founding member rate. They're your core community and will set the tone.
  5. 5
    Run parallel for a transition period. Keep Discord active but redirect new conversations to Skool. "Great question! I answered this in detail over in Skool: [link]"
  6. 6
    Archive Discord. Set it to read-only once the transition is complete. Don't delete it entirely in case members want to reference old conversations.
